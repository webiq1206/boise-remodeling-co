# Boise Remodeling Co house bible

Brand: Boise Remodeling Co  
House: 1972 Boise Bench ranch  
Authorized: Phase 10 of the P5 Family Website Fix Plan (28 October 2026)

## Architecture
- Single-storey ranch, 1972
- Red-brown brick wainscot below sage-grey horizontal lap siding
- Crisp white fascia and trim
- Low-pitched 4:12 hip roof, medium grey architectural shingles
- New black-framed single-hung windows
- New black front door with three square lites
- Small gabled porch with square white columns
- Attached two-car garage, white carriage-style door

## Site
- Mature Boise Bench street
- Large maples and a blue spruce
- Irrigated lawn, sidewalk and curb
- Concrete driveway
- Dry Boise foothills visible at the end of the street

## Interior spine
- Hickory plank floor through the main level
- White walls, flat-stock white trim
- White shaker cabinets, sage green island, white quartz
- Brushed brass pulls and faucet
- Black-framed windows matching the exterior

## Camera
- 35 mm equivalent, eye level, straight verticals
- Late afternoon high-desert light, long soft shadows
- Same season and white balance across the set
