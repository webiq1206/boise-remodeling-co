CMYK varies by press profile — take those values from your own swatch file or your printer, not from a screen conversion.

The accent is used in exactly two places: the seal's outer ring and dots, and the wordmark's Co. Nothing else in the identity is sage.

Sage holds on either ground — 5.00:1 on charcoal, 2.46:1 on bone. It has most presence reversed. Don't set accent-colored text on bone.

Full wordmark

The stacked lockup — name, rule, tagline, location. Use it where the mark can stand alone with room around it: letterhead, the head of a proposal, a truck panel, an email signature footer. The rule carries the accent, as it does on the seal.

Small-size mark

Below about 160 px the seal stops reading, so this is a separately drawn mark — not a scaled one. The accent is a solid band at 5.9% of the diameter, where the seal's ring is 0.22%, and the letterform is the script initial from Remodeling. Use it for favicons, app icons and social avatars.

Clear space: 8% of the seal's diameter on all sides; for the wordmark, a margin equal to the cap height of the B. Below 160px the seal's arc text stops reading — use the wordmark instead.

Do / don't

  • Use a disc version over photography — the mono seals need a clean, even field.
  • Keep the accent to the outer ring, the dots, and Co.
  • Never recolor outside charcoal, bone, and #9AA098.
  • Never set sage type on bone.
  • Never stretch, rotate, or condense either lockup.
  • Never mix another company's accent with the Boise Remodeling Co name.
