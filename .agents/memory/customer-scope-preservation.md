---
name: Customer scope preservation
description: Disclosure and scope-safety checks need positive construction counterexamples, not only rejection tests.
---

Treat preservation of priced scope as a separate acceptance condition from hiding financial internals.

**Why:** A disclosure repair passed negative leakage checks but removed an overhead garage-door line while retaining its selling total. Blanket owner-supply and shared-word alternate guards also misclassified legitimate mixed responsibility and competing selections.

**How to apply:** Pair every restrictive guard with actual pricing/output counterexamples: ordinary construction vocabulary, mixed quantities assigned to different suppliers, and alternatives sharing generic trade words. Assert both the exact retained scope/quantities and the absence of prohibited charges. Conflicting evidence must remain unresolved rather than being inferred from one keyword.
