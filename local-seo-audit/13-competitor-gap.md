## Gap analysis: them vs us

### Where competitors win today
1. **Reviews**: Renaissance 55+, Exquisite 86 (Houzz), Pristine 46 vs **our 0**. This is the entire Local Pack game and it is gated on GBP verification (02/08 plans).
2. **Published credentials**: PlanPro and Ellett print their RCE registration numbers on-page; CCH leads with NARI awards. We print Idaho Registered Contractor #8381215. Direct confirmation of the 11-eeat-audit.md recommendation.
3. **Named humans**: Pristine's "Zach", Freimark's founder, PlanPro's principal appear in content and reviews. Our site had zero named people until this pass (founder section added).
4. **Houzz presence**: the Meridian kitchen/bath SERP is heavily mediated by Houzz pack pages (88 pros listed). We are not on Houzz at all. Tier-2 citation priority confirmed.
5. **Video**: Renaissance embeds an ADU build video. We have none.
6. **ADU specialists exist**: PlanPro, Mode Homes, Freimark all run dedicated ADU operations with strong pages - the keyword is contested, not empty. *(Corrects the earlier hypothesis that ADU coverage was thin.)*

### Where we now win or can win
1. **Content architecture**: none of these competitors has a 10-hub, 94-piece pillar/cluster system with FAQ/schema scaffolding. Renaissance's content is service pages + portfolio; the Houzz players have no content moat at all.
2. **Structured cost transparency**: nobody in the field publishes structured, dated, local cost-band tables. Our cost guide + new server-rendered service cost bands are unique in the market - the most defensible GEO asset available.
3. **Neighborhood coverage**: only Renaissance mentions historic districts; nobody has neighborhood remodeling guides (North End, Bench, Harris Ranch). Our expanded neighborhood set is a first-mover asset.
4. **Permit content**: the Ada-vs-Canyon permit resource has no competitor equivalent.
5. **ADU angle vs specialists**: the ADU specialists are new-build-focused. Our wedge, now expressed in the ADU pillar: ADU + main-house remodel as one design-build program (specialists can't), garage conversions as remodel-native work, and pre-approved-plan guidance.
6. **Schema/AEO depth**: BBB-syndicated schema aside, no competitor approaches our JSON-LD graph, Speakable, llms.txt, or quick-answer formatting.
