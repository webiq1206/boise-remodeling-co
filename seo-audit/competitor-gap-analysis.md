## Where competitors LEAD (gaps to close)

| Dimension | Competitor strength | BRC gap |
|---|---|---|
| **Named people / E-E-A-T** | Renaissance (Chad & Shelley), Apex (John & Theresa), Ethos (Sarah) | BRC has **no named people** |
| **Reviews & ratings** | Strite 114 reviews; multiple 4.9–5.0 Houzz | BRC `rating: 0, reviewCount: 0` |
| **Awards / associations** | NARI CotY, Best of Houzz, "Idaho's Best Remodeler" | BRC has none surfaced |
| **Longevity proof** | 1975 / 1997 / 2004 founding dates touted | BRC founding year: leave blank until documented. Does not emphasize track record/volume |
| **Warranty specificity** | Boyd "3-year warranty" | BRC "written workmanship guarantee" (unspecified term) |
| **Showroom / tangible** | Apex showroom | BRC virtual-only (acceptable for design-build) |
| **Third-party profiles** | Houzz/GuildQuality/Google heavily used | BRC `sameAs` = FB/IG only |

## Strategic recommendations

1. **Close the people gap** — the clearest, highest-impact differentiator competitors exploit. Name founder + 1–2 leads with bios/credentials. (data-gated; plumbing wired)
2. **Activate reviews/ratings** — competitors win "best remodeler Boise" on visible ratings; BRC's superior content can't rank for comparative/authority queries without them. (data-gated; Review schema wired)
3. **Pursue + surface NARI Idaho membership / awards** — table stakes for authority in this market.
4. **Specify the warranty term** (e.g. "X-year workmanship guarantee") to match Boyd's concrete claim.
5. **Add Houzz + Google + GuildQuality to `sameAs`** once profiles exist.
6. **Lean into the content moat** — BRC's content/topical authority already exceeds competitors; pairing it with real trust signals would make BRC the most complete local entity.

## Net assessment

BRC has a **stronger content and technical-SEO foundation than any single competitor**, but **weaker visible trust/authority signals than all of the established players**. The competitive opportunity is unusual: the hard part (deep, well-structured content + schema) is done; the missing part (named people, reviews, awards, profiles) is largely data-entry once the real-world facts are supplied.
